TacTicCube

A colorful, three-dimensional twist on the classic tic-tac-toe — rotate, slice, and outsmart your opponent on every side of the cube.

What is TacTicCube?

TacTicCube takes the game you already know and turns it into a real 3D puzzle. Instead of one flat grid, you're playing across a rotating cube — every face, every angle, every layer matters. Line up your pieces before your opponent does, play against a friend, or challenge the built-in AI at a difficulty level that grows with you.
