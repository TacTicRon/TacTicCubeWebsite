1. The App

TacTicCube is a 3D tic-tac-toe puzzle game for iOS, iPadOS, and Windows, designed to help players of all ages — especially kids — practice math and strategic thinking.

2. Accounts and Profiles

TacTicCube does not require creating an account with an email address or password. Players choose a nickname to play. Progress is tied to a randomly-generated, encrypted device identifier, not to any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

3. Purchases

  • Free tier: TacTicCube is free to play, supported by ads on iOS/iPadOS. The Windows version has no ads.

  • Before any purchase can begin, the App shows a parental gate (a simple math problem) in addition to Apple's own purchase protections, since TacTicCube is listed in Apple's Kids Category.

4. Referral Program

TacTicCube includes an in-house referral program that rewards players for inviting others to the App, built entirely on encrypted, anonymous identifiers (never personal information).

  • Referral commissions are calculated across a five-level chain. The fifth level is always donated to Camp Gratitude, a nonprofit supporting veterans and military families, regardless of whether that slot is filled.
  • Levels one through four are only paid out to a referring player who currently holds a Pro subscription. If one of those slots is empty, or the referring player at that level doesn't currently have Pro, that share is simply not paid out — MoonLight Industries retains it rather than donating it. A player who does hold an eligible slot may choose to voluntarily donate their own earned share to Camp Gratitude as well, in addition to (not instead of) the Level 5 donation.
  • Commissions are provisional for a holding period (currently 30 days, subject to change at our discretion) before becoming final, to account for refunds.
  • If a purchase behind a commission is refunded after that commission has already been paid out, the amount will be deducted from that referrer's next payout.
  • We may change, suspend, or discontinue the referral program, or adjust its terms (including the holding period), at any time.
  • Any attempt to manipulate, abuse, or defraud the referral program (such as fake accounts or self-referrals) may result in forfeiture of commissions and suspension of access to the App.
